01 / ABOUT

A place for practical engineering

Stephen's Lab is a hands-on engineering portfolio focused on understanding how modern applications are built, deployed, monitored and operated.

Each project aims to document not only what was built, but also why particular tools and approaches were chosen, how the result was tested, and what could be improved.
